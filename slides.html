01 · Who & pain

Churn is public before it's on the renewal call

The signals that precede a lost account sit in the open — a competitor's changelog quietly shipping an integration with your customer, an engineering post about an in-house replacement, a job req for the team that makes you redundant, an acquirer who already has a vendor for what you sell.

Nobody on the CS team has time to look, and the accounts nobody checks are exactly the ones that surprise you at renewal.

02 · Why keyword tools can't see this

The best churn evidence never contains the word churn

product.hubspot.com · Jun 2026
HubSpot engineering ships “Vector-as-a-Service” — an internal retrieval platform now serving 20 billion vectors.
keyword alert: silent — never says “Exa”
tavily.com/blog · May 2026
A competitor's changelog: “Connect Tavily to Devin through the MCP Marketplace…” — your customer, on their product surface.
keyword alert: silent — wrong site, wrong words
cnbc.com · Aug 2026
“Stripe agrees to acquire OpenRouter” — the acquirer consolidates procurement; the renewal owner changes overnight.
semantic sweep: flagged, dated, cited

These require searching by meaning — “this account is building what we sell” — scoped by domain (competitors' sites only) and date. That's Exa: neural search + includeDomains scoping + structured summaries + findSimilar, composed into five signal lanes per account.
